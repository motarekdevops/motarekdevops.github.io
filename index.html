MasterOps, a package manager for DevOps projects

A command-line tool that sets up a project’s infrastructure, Docker, Kubernetes, Nginx, CI/CD and security in one go, on a laptop or a server, in the spirit of composer create-project. Now at v0.1.15.

  • Installed with apt from a private registry. Every push to main builds the .deb and publishes it with no manual release.
  • Commands for new, doctor, start, nginx, status, audit, backup, vault, diff, upgrade and cleanup.
  • Terraform modules for VPC, EC2, IAM, S3 and Route 53, with a guard that refuses to apply from a live EC2 instance.
  • Ansible runs underneath as the provisioning engine. Stacks cover Postgres, MySQL, Laravel, Django, Go, .NET and Spring Boot.

UX strategies

The principles I apply to every interface, including this page.

  • Lead with the main job. The first screen answers who I am and what I do.
  • Reveal detail on demand. Summaries first, depth one click away.
  • Name actions plainly. Buttons say what happens next.

  • Accessible by default. Visible focus, readable contrast, reduced motion respected.
  • One system, many screens. Shared color, type and spacing tokens keep it consistent.
